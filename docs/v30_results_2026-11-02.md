# v30 results — SAC image-goal navigation in CARLA

All numbers are for **v30 step_300000** unless a row says otherwise. Every
evaluation is deterministic (the policy's mean action) and **fault-terminal**:
a lane crossing, leaving the road, or a collision ends the episode as a failure.
Success = within the arrival radius at ≤ 10 km/h. "Wrong goal" = the same
episode (same seed, start and true goal) driven with another episode's goal
image, taken ≥ 5 m away; it measures how much success depends on the image.
Comparisons are paired per episode (exact two-sided McNemar on the discordant
pairs); 95% CIs are Wilson, computed at full precision (the eval
harness's printed CI double-rounds, e.g. 0.0845 -> 0.09; use these).

## 1. Main result: the goal image is used, and it transfers

Standing evaluation set: goals 5–25 m along the road (in practice ~20–25 m,
see caveat 6), 85% placed past a junction, arrival radius 2.0 m. Both towns
held out from training (trained on Town05 + Town01); Town03 never seen at all.

| Town | n | True goal SR | SPL | Wrong goal SR | Gap | True-only / wrong-only | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Town02 | 100 | **0.560** [0.46, 0.65] | 0.524 | 0.030 [0.01, 0.08] | **+0.530** | 53 / 0 | < 0.0001 |
| Town03 | 100 | **0.350** [0.26, 0.45] | 0.341 | 0.040 [0.02, 0.10] | **+0.310** | 31 / 0 | < 0.0001 |

Failure modes differ by arm, consistent with goal-directed steering: with a
wrong image the car fails by driving somewhere else (Town02: 67/100 end on a
lane crossing vs 22/100 with the true image; Town03: 53/100 off-road vs 29/100).

## 2. Ablation: the charge for driving away from the goal

Same reward otherwise; only `ROUTE_RETREAT_SCALE` (what a metre driven away
from the goal costs, relative to the +1 a metre towards it pays) changes.
Town02, n = 100 per arm, best checkpoint of each run.

| Run | Retreat charge | True | Wrong | Gap | True-only / wrong-only | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v27 | 1.0 | 0.470 [0.38, 0.57] | 0.300 [0.22, 0.40] | +0.170 | 24 / 7 | 0.0033 |
| v29 | 0.0 | 0.480 [0.38, 0.58] | 0.470 [0.38, 0.57] | +0.010 | 7 / 6 | 1.00 |
| **v30** | **0.3** | **0.560** [0.46, 0.65] | **0.030** [0.01, 0.09] | **+0.530** | 53 / 0 | < 0.0001 |

- **Full charge (v27):** the policy reads the image, but a lost car's value goes
  negative (goal Q −20 to −48), so ending the episode on a −1 crossing becomes
  the best move and the run collapses.
- **No charge (v29):** stable, but the image is ignored: success with a wrong
  image (0.470) equals success with the right one (0.480) — lane following
  that collects goals lying ahead.
- **0.3 (v30):** navigates by the image and stays stable to its peak.

A success rate alone cannot separate navigation from lane following: v29's
0.480 looks like v30's 0.560 until the wrong-goal control is run.

## 3. Long-distance benchmark (Gibson/Mezghani-style protocol)

Mirrors the Mezghani ImageNav protocol a peer runs on Gibson (fixed episode
set, difficulty by geodesic distance, fixed count per difficulty per scene,
SR/SPL, paired arms with Holm correction), scaled to CARLA.

- **Scenes:** 4 held-out towns — Town02, Town03, Town04, Town10HD (Town06/07
  are not in the cluster's CARLA image).
- **Episodes:** 100 per difficulty per town = **1,200 per arm**, fixed (episode
  file `logs/bench_long_episodes.json` on the cluster).
- **Difficulty by ROUTE distance:** easy 10–25 m, medium 25–50 m, hard
  50–100 m. Each difficulty is generated from its own distance band, and an
  episode counts only if its planner route distance falls in its bin.
- **Success** at Gibson's **1.0 m** radius and at our training radius
  **2.0 m**, scored from one run. No STOP action (see caveat 4).
- **NRNS split:** straight if route / straight-line distance ≤ 1.20.

**By difficulty**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy (10–25 m) | 400 | **0.217** | **0.395** | 0.209 | 0.013 / 0.022 | 87 / 5 | < 0.0001 |
| Medium (25–50 m) | 400 | 0.120 | 0.220 | 0.118 | 0.003 / 0.007 | 47 / 0 | < 0.0001 |
| Hard (50–100 m) | 400 | 0.022 | 0.062 | 0.022 | 0.000 / 0.005 | 9 / 0 | 0.0039 |
| **All** | 1,200 | **0.120** | **0.226** | 0.116 | 0.005 / 0.012 | 143 / 5 | < 0.0001 |

**By town**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Town02 | 300 | 0.243 | 0.340 | 0.236 | 0.010 | < 0.0001 |
| Town03 | 300 | 0.073 | 0.180 | 0.071 | 0.000 | < 0.0001 |

| Town10HD | 300 | 0.073 | 0.150 | 0.071 | 0.010 | 0.0002 |

Readings:
1. Goal-reading holds at every distance and in every town (wrong-goal SR ≈ 0).
2. Success falls steeply with distance for a policy trained on goals ≤ 25 m;
   the hard bin is where a map built from exploration would have to help.
3. Gibson's 1 m radius roughly halves SR relative to the 2 m training radius.
4. Curved routes essentially never succeed (1 of 179): the policy reaches
   goals ahead along the road, not routes that bend away.

Context only, not a head-to-head: DEBiT-B on Gibson (Mezghani, 4,200 episodes,
peer's run) scores 97.0 / 92.1 / 83.3 / 90.8% SR, SPL 0.625 — a different
domain and scale, with explicit STOP.

## 5. Caveats for the paper

1. **Peak checkpoint, not a converged policy.** Results are for step 300k;
   later checkpoints lose goal-reading (§4).
2. **The 0.3 retreat charge is exploitable.** Circling a block approaches and
   leaves the goal through legal junction turns without crossing a line. A
   resumed copy (v32) collapsed to 0.000 by 200k doing this (13/40 episodes
   drove ~240 m for the whole clock, returning up to +95 without arriving), and
   v30 at its peak already had crossing episodes returning +41 on 25 m routes.
   The §1 numbers stand as measured — the paired control rules out lane
   following — but the reward is not farm-proof. A "new ground only" progress
   term (v33+) removes the exploit.
3. **Training goals were 5–25 m**; the long bins test generalisation in distance.
4. **No STOP action.** "Stop" = arriving at ≤ 10 km/h; attempts to train an
   explicit declare action never learned to declare. Gibson requires STOP.
5. **Four held-out towns**, not six (Town06/07 absent from the image).
6. **The standing set's goals are mostly ~20–25 m**, not uniform over 5–25 m:
   85% of starts sit before a junction and the past-junction rule keeps only
   distances ≥ 15 m past it. The §3 benchmark generates each bin from its own
   band to avoid this.
7. Per-checkpoint curves (§4) use n = 40; the headline tables use n = 100+.
